**[Personal Computational Projects — 2026](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/manchester-msc-projects)**\
Four projects I developed in 2026, informed by my MSc studies. Each has its own code, documentation and evaluated results; all published results come from these 2026 versions, and no module mark refers to them.

- **[Surrogate-Assisted Screening of CO₂ Injection Schedules with Simulator Verification](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/manchester-msc-projects/tree/main/advanced-subsurface-modelling/SubsurfaceML)**: a CO₂ injection simulator with machine-learning surrogates that predict peak pressure build-up in 100 synthetic test reservoirs generated after the design was frozen (RMSE 0.31 MPa).
- **[Implicit Finite-Difference Modelling of Transient Heat Conduction in an Anisotropic Plate](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/manchester-msc-projects/tree/main/fundamentals-of-numerical-modelling-and-simulation/anisotropic-heat-conduction)**: a sparse backward-Euler solver, verified against exact and worked solutions, with separate grid and time-step studies.
- **[Neural Surrogate for the Two-Phase Flash Calculation](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/manchester-msc-projects/tree/main/properties-of-subsurface-fluids/Subsurface_DL_Project)**: PyTorch networks that reproduce the Wilson / Rachford-Rice flash for unseen mixtures in a synthetic benchmark, trained with and without the Rachford-Rice residual in the loss.
- **[Testing Gardner's Velocity–Density Relation against Measured Well Logs](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/manchester-msc-projects/tree/main/subsurface-mechanics-and-geoengineering/SeisGeoMech)**: on measured sonic and density logs from a UK North Sea well, the relation under-predicts density by 3.5 %; the error is traced into a one-dimensional overburden-stress (ρg) gradient and synthetic seismic reflectivity.

**[CO₂ Storage — Research Interests](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/co2-storage-research-interest)**\
Proposed research directions: how reliably pressure and CO₂ plume migration can be predicted through injection and after shut-in, and what pressure and seismic monitoring can confirm. Four illustrative notebooks show the planned methods on toy examples and an untrained network; they are not research results, and the full studies are future work.
