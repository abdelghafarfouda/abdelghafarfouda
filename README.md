**[MSc Projects — University of Manchester](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/manchester-msc-projects)**\
Completed MSc module projects, each with its own code, documentation and evaluated results: an implicit finite-difference solver for anisotropic heat conduction; a CO₂ injection simulator with machine-learning surrogates that predict peak pressure build-up in 100 synthetic test reservoirs generated after the design was frozen (RMSE 0.31 MPa); a neural surrogate for two-phase flash calculations; and a test of Gardner's velocity–density relation against measured sonic and density logs from a UK North Sea well, where it under-predicts density by 3.5 %, with the error traced into a one-dimensional overburden-stress (ρg) gradient and synthetic seismic reflectivity.

**[CO₂ Storage — Research Interests](https://github.com/abdelghafarfouda/co2-storage-research-interest)**\
Proposed research directions: how reliably pressure and CO₂ plume migration can be predicted through injection and after shut-in, and what pressure and seismic monitoring can confirm. Four illustrative notebooks show the planned methods on toy examples and an untrained network; they are not research results, and the full studies are future work.
